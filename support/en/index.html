No. 01

How Scanvy reads labels

Frame the label and take the photo. The iPhone reads the text and barcodes inside the frame by itself, with Apple's built-in recognition. Characters that are easy to mix up, like 0 and O or 8 and B, are marked whenever the phone isn't sure.

If cloud reading is on, those unsure labels get a second reading: only the framed area goes to our server and to Claude, made by Anthropic, and the text comes back. With cloud reading off, the phone reads alone and asks you to check the marked characters.

No. 02

Getting a clean read

  • Hold the phone square to the label and let the label fill most of the frame.
  • Glare hides characters. Tilt the label a little or turn on the light.
  • One photo can hold several labels. Every number in it is read, and it still counts as one read.
  • If a value comes out wrong, open the item and correct it. Your correction is saved in the library.

No. 03

Reads and the daily limit

A read is one photo, with every number on it. The free plan has 3 a day, plus one extra the first time. The count resets at your local midnight. When you run out, you can keep taking photos: they wait in your library and are read the next day, or right away with Pro. A read that fails is given back.

No. 04

Turning cloud reading on or off

Open More and use the "Second reading in the cloud" switch under Reading. It is off unless you turned it on. In a few countries and regions cloud reading isn't available; the app then reads on the phone and tells you so. The privacy policy lists exactly what is sent.

No. 05

Without an internet connection

You can keep taking photos offline. Each read needs a quick check with our server, so the photos wait in the library and are read once you're back online.

No. 06

Buying, restoring and cancelling Pro

  • To buy, open More and tap "See Scanvy Pro". If you bought it before with the same Apple Account, tap Restore on that screen.
  • To cancel, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Scanvy Pro. Do it at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • Refunds come from Apple. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com; we can't issue App Store refunds ourselves.

No. 07

Getting your list out

You can export your library as CSV at any time, on the free plan too. Pro adds Excel and PDF. The library lives only on this iPhone, so export it before you switch phones or delete the app.

No. 08

Deleting your data

In More, "Delete my account and server data" removes what our server keeps about you. The library on your iPhone stays; delete the app to remove it. A subscription has to be cancelled separately in Settings.

No. 09

Contact

Write to redpersoncoding@gmail.com. It helps to include the app version (shown under More), your iPhone model and your iOS version. Please don't attach photos of labels that show personal details.
