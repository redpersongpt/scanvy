No. 01

Apple's standard license

Your use of Scanvy is covered by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (the Standard EULA). The sections below add the details about plans, cloud reading and fair use. Where they and the Standard EULA disagree, the Standard EULA applies.

No. 02

Free use

Scanvy is free to download. Without a subscription you get 3 reads a day, plus one extra read the first time you use the app. One read is one photo, with every number on it. The count resets at midnight in the time zone your account was opened in. Reads that fail don't count. Taking photos, using your library and exporting to CSV are always free.

No. 03

Scanvy Pro

Scanvy Pro is an auto-renewing subscription with two options:

  • Weekly: $4.99 a week, with 1,000 cloud reads in each paid week.
  • Yearly: $39.99 a year, with 1,500 cloud reads in each month, counted from the day you bought it.

These are US prices. The App Store shows the price for your country before you buy, and that is the price you pay. Reading on the phone has no limit on either plan, and Pro adds Excel and PDF export and bulk import. There is no free trial.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Family Sharing isn't available for Scanvy Pro. One subscription can be restored on up to 5 of your own devices; they share the same cloud reads rather than adding them up.

No. 04

Fair use of cloud reading

Every cloud read has a real cost, which is why Pro has the caps above. When you reach one, nothing gets locked: you keep taking photos and the phone keeps reading. Labels the phone isn't sure about are shown to you to check until the next week or month begins. If our daily cloud budget runs out, cloud reading can pause for everyone until the next day, and the phone keeps reading in the meantime.

Please don't send reads with automated tools, try to get around the limits, or use cloud reading for anything other than reading labels in the app. We may limit or close accounts that do.

No. 05

Read results

Scanvy reads printed characters, and wear, glare and small print can fool any reader. Characters that look alike, such as 0 and O, are marked when the app isn't sure. Check a number before you rely on it for something that matters, like a warranty claim or an asset register. Scanvy is provided as it is, as the Standard EULA describes.

No. 06

Availability

Scanvy isn't offered in mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau or Russia. Cloud reading works only where Anthropic offers Claude; everywhere else the phone reads on its own. Our server may sometimes be offline for maintenance.

No. 07

Your content

The photos and lists you make are yours. What you send through cloud reading is used only to read it for you, as the privacy policy explains.

No. 08
